# DeckLab sample artwork

Neo Dino Runner is a 232 × 50 GIF with 160 frames at 20 fps (eight seconds). Four transparent dinosaur poses were generated with an image-generation tool, reduced to pixel sprites and assembled with a scrolling ground and clouds. The prompt requested a simple monochrome T-rex runner inspired by the classic offline browser game; no Google game assets were copied into this package.

Editable sprite poses are in `dino-source/`. Run `python3 render_dino.py` with Pillow installed to regenerate the bundled GIF. The GIF, source poses and generator are supplied under the project’s MPL-2.0 licence (see ../../LICENSE). This records provenance and does not imply Google or Elgato endorsement.

The bundled catalogue is independent of My artwork storage. Placed images are embedded in exported profiles and retain their original animation bytes. Physical Neo playback is not yet validated.
