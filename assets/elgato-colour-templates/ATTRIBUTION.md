The owner supplied `Stream Deck Template Color.zip` from this resource. DeckLab bundles its 38 artwork sheets unchanged; the Light example and four section-heading PNGs are omitted from the picker. `index.json` records the archive hash, each sheet hash, source dimensions and crop rectangles.

DeckLab crops key/panel artwork from the sheets, selects colour/tone variants and resizes or tiles them for the chosen device surface. These runtime adaptations do not change control assignments or hardware geometry. The original Stroke/Blue.png, Stroke/Gray.png and Stroke/Gray-1.png contain purple, blue and gray artwork respectively; displayed labels are corrected while original filenames and bytes are preserved.

The artwork and adaptations retain CC BY 4.0; MPL 2.0 covers DeckLab's own code. Attribution travels with template images saved to My artwork as pack/author/licence metadata, and a compact record travels with applied template recipes in DeckLab profiles. No endorsement by the creators or CORSAIR is implied.
